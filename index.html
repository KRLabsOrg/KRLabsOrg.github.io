About Us

KR Labs develops transparent and trustworthy AI solutions for natural language processing (NLP) tasks. KR Labs was founded by NLP researchers Ádám Kovács and Gábor Recski. Our open-source technology is based on a novel combination of statistical and rule-based methods developed at TUW-NLP and HLT-Budapest. Between 2023 and 2024, KR Labs was incubated in the TU Wien Innovation Incubation Center (i²c).

Our Products

LettuceDetect

LettuceDetect is a hallucination detection framework designed for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. Built on ModernBERT, it identifies unsupported segments in LLM-generated answers in real time. It overcomes the limitations of short context windows and the high computational costs associated with large LLM-based detectors.

  • Token-Level Detection: Flags unsupported parts of answers.
  • Long-Context Support: Processes up to 4K tokens.
  • High Throughput: 30–60 examples per second on a single GPU.
  • Open Source: Released under the MIT License.
