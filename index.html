[lettucedetect]

Hallucination detection for AI outputs. Flags ungrounded claims before they ship.

Span-level detection of unsupported content in RAG answers, generated code, and agentic tool output. Compact encoder models that outperform prompt-based judges at a fraction of the cost, in seven languages.

GitHub· Docs· PyPI· Models· Paper

[verbatim]

Retrieval-augmented generation with span-level attribution.

VerbatimRAG prevents hallucination by construction: extractive models select the relevant source spans, and the LLM only fills templates around them, so every part of an answer traces back to a verbatim source span.

GitHub· ACL Anthology demo

[rulechef]

Learn rule-based models from examples using LLM-powered synthesis.

You provide labeled examples, RuleChef generates regex, code, or spaCy rules, and those rules run locally with no LLM at inference time: fast, deterministic, and inspectable.

GitHub· Docs· PyPI
